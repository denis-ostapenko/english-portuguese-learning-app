# Learning Recommendations

## Recommended daily routine

Study for 20 to 30 minutes on most days:

- 5 minutes of due reviews;
- 10 new items or fewer when the review queue is large;
- one situation in listening mode;
- 3 to 5 minutes of speaking aloud.

Consistency matters more than a large one-time session.

## Use audio before text

For every third review, look away from the screen and identify the item from audio. Replay no more than twice before showing the answer.

## Produce complete phrases

Do not practice an isolated word only. Read its example and create one personal sentence with the same pattern.

## Keep the new-item load realistic

Ten new items per day is a strong default. Reduce the goal when more than 40 reviews are due. Increase it only when recall remains comfortable for a full week.

## Shadow situations

Listen to one line and repeat it immediately with the same rhythm. Accuracy is less important than maintaining the phrase as one unit.

## Mark familiar items honestly

Use the familiar-item control only when you can understand the item in natural speech and produce it without reading. Recognition alone is not mastery.

## Review progress weekly

Export a backup once a week. Check whether the number of due reviews is stable. A rapidly growing queue means the daily new-item goal is too high.
